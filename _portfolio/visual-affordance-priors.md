This project studies how robots can select effective dual-grasp pairs for dynamic garment flattening from a single RGB observation.

We combine high-throughput physics simulation, structural pair-value learning on 3D garment point clouds, and dense image-space affordance distillation. The resulting visual prior transfers to real garments without fine-tuning on real data.

**Status:** Manuscript under review at IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.
