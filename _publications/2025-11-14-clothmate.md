ClothMate starts from a simple observation: garment flattening by flinging can be partly decoupled from the detailed garment state once the robot has a useful grasp. We use this grasp-fling consistency to build a two-stage value-learning framework that reduces the data requirement to 15% of category-specific baselines while a single model supports shirts, dresses, pants, jumpsuits, and skirts.
