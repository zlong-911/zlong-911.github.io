This project studies how robots can select effective dual-grasp pairs for dynamic garment flattening from a single RGB observation.

We develop a structural-to-visual value-learning framework that combines high-throughput physics simulation, structural pair-value learning on 3D garment point clouds, and dense image-space affordance distillation. The resulting visual prior transfers to real garments without fine-tuning on real data.

The system improves zero-shot transfer on the aRTF-Clothes benchmark and increases real-robot unfolding quality across 22 garment assets.
