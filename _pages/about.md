I am a master's student in the School of Automation Science and Engineering at [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/en/). My research focuses on enabling robots to perceive and manipulate deformable objects, with a particular interest in generalizable garment manipulation.

I work at the intersection of **robot manipulation**, **deformable object manipulation**, **computer vision**, and **sim-to-real transfer**. My recent work studies data-efficient garment flattening and visual affordance learning from large-scale simulation.

### Research interests

- Robot manipulation
- Deformable object manipulation
- Visual affordance learning
- Sim-to-real transfer
